## What happens

Something exists to save you on a bad day (a backup job, certificate renewal, an alert
rule, a failover), and its *definition* is checked ("the job exists", "the timer is
enabled"). Its *outcome* isn't. The net fails silently, and you find out on the day you
fall.

## How to spot it

Ask of each protection: what is the newest proof that it worked, and what tells me when that
proof gets old?

- Backups: age of the newest archive per guest
  (`pvesh get /nodes/pve01/storage/local/content --content backup`), not job membership
  (`pve_not_backed_up_*` only says a guest is *in* a job).
- Certificates: `probe_ssl_earliest_cert_expiry`, not "certbot.timer is enabled".
- Alerts: did a test alert reach a person?
- A restore, a failover or a rebuild that has never been done counts as not working.

## How to fix

Measure the result and alert on its age: `time() - <last success timestamp> > <period +
margin>`. Then exercise the net once on purpose (a test restore, a forced renewal with
`--dry-run`, a test alert) and write down how it went.

## How to prevent

When adding any protection, add its "last success" metric and alert in the same change,
plus a KB note of the first deliberate test.
