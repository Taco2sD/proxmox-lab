# Nested virtualization behaves differently from bare metal

## What happens

Proxmox runs inside a Hyper-V Gen 2 VM. Installers, drivers and clocks assume real hardware or a common hypervisor: synthetic devices appear late, workarounds get persisted by installers, and guests freeze or drift with their host.

## How to spot it

- An installer finds no disk or keyboard on Hyper-V (synthetic storage and keyboard modules not loaded).
- Boot parameters you added by hand reappear in `/etc/default/grub.d/`.
- Guest clocks far off after the host was busy or asleep: `chronyc tracking`, the ClockSkew alert.

## How to fix

Load the missing modules early (scripted GRUB command line), clean up what the installer persisted, and let chrony step large offsets at any time (`makestep 1 -1`).

## How to prevent

Script every manual workaround in host-setup/ so it's repeatable, and watch clock offset on every machine.
