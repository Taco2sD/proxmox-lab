# An alert counts history instead of current state

## What happens

An alert built on "how many X happened in the last N hours" keeps firing after the cause is fixed,
until old events age out of the window. People learn to ignore it, and then it hides a new one.

## How to spot it

- The alert fires, but what it names works again (the latest run succeeded).
- The expression counts over a window (`increase(...[24h])`, a "last 24 h" gauge) and says nothing
  about whether the latest attempt succeeded.

## How to fix

Alert on current state: "the most recent attempt of this kind failed and nothing has succeeded
since", exported as its own metric. Keep the counts for dashboards, where trends belong.

## How to prevent

Review every new alert with one question: once someone has fixed this, does the alert stop by
itself within minutes? If not, it alerts on history.
