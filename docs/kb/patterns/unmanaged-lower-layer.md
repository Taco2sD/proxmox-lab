## What happens

Everything above a certain layer is in git, monitored and reconciled. The layer below it
(the Windows host and Hyper-V under pve01, the VPS at the edge, the ISP or Wi-Fi) isn't. It
updates, reboots, sleeps or changes on its own, and every managed layer above takes the hit
at once, with symptoms that point at the wrong place (clock skew, a dropped tunnel, every
target down).

## How to spot it

Many unrelated alerts at the same moment (TargetDown on everything, ClockSkew on every
host, TunnelDown) mean a lower layer. Check the host's uptime and pending reboots
(`Component Based Servicing\RebootPending`), the hypervisor VM's stop and start actions,
and the provider's maintenance notices.

## How to fix

Give the layer an owner and a maintenance window: reboot it deliberately when it suits you,
pause automatic updates around important dates, and record its configuration (scripts in
host-setup/, vps/) like any other.

## How to prevent

Monitor at least the lower layer's heartbeat from outside it, and keep a "known lower-layer
events" note per maintenance window.
