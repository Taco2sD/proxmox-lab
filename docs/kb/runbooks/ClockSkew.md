# ClockSkew

## What it means

A machine's clock has been over half a second away from its time source for 5 minutes.
Kerberos (every AD sign-in) refuses clocks 5 minutes apart, and TLS breaks with large
errors. Workstations take their time from dc01 (roles/base); mgmt01 is a container and
runs on pve01's clock.

## First five minutes

1. On the host: `chronyc tracking` (System time, Last offset) and `chronyc sources -v`.
2. Is the source right? `chronyc tracking` on dc01 and on pve01.
3. The Clock offset panel on the Lab overview: one machine, or everyone together?

## Common causes

- The VMs were frozen with their host (the Windows host slept or was overloaded) and
  woke up behind. roles/base sets `makestep 1 -1` (/etc/chrony/conf.d/vm-makestep.conf)
  so chrony steps the clock at once instead of slewing for hours: check the file is there.
- dc01 can't reach its own upstream, so everyone drifts together.
- mgmt01 is off: fix pve01's clock, not the container's.

## Escalate / fix

- Step it now with `chronyc makestep`, then confirm with `chronyc tracking`.
