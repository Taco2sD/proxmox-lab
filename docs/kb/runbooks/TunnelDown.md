# TunnelDown

## What it means

The WireGuard tunnel from pve01 to the VPS has had no handshake for over 5 minutes. The
tunnel is the internet's only way in (roles/portal_relay): https://lab.radicallydecent.com
and https://pve.radicallydecent.com are unreachable from outside, while everything inside
the lab keeps working. The metric comes from `lab-wg-metrics.timer` on pve01
(roles/pve_monitoring).

## First five minutes

1. On pve01: `wg show lab` (latest handshake, transfer), `systemctl status wg-quick@lab`,
   `ping -c3 10.99.0.1` (the VPS's end of the tunnel).
2. Can pve01 reach the internet at all? `ping -c3 1.1.1.1`. If not, the Windows host
   (the lab's NAT gateway, on Wi-Fi) is the problem.
3. The VPS side: the "Public sign-in (VPS)" probe; on the VPS (`ssh rd`), `sudo wg show`
   and `systemctl status wg-quick@lab` (vps/README.md).

## Common causes

- The Windows host lost its network or went to sleep.
- The VPS rebooted and its end of the tunnel didn't come back.
- The tunnel came up without its firewall: its PostUp loads /etc/wireguard/lab.nft; check
  `nft list table inet wglab` on pve01.

## Escalate / fix

- pve01: `systemctl restart wg-quick@lab`, then `wg show lab` until a handshake appears.
- The VPS also hosts the website: on it, touch only the tunnel.
